The official web portal and client lead-generation platform for **The Remedy Group**, a specialized US healthcare medical billing and Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) agency founded by **Muhammad Jawad**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **💼 Comprehensive RCM Service Showcase**: Dedicated breakdowns for Full-Cycle Billing, ICD-10/CPT Medical Coding, Denial Management, Aging A/R Recovery, and Patient Eligibility Verification.
- **🖥️ Multidisciplinary EHR Compatibility**: Highlights expertise across leading US healthcare platforms including **eClinicalWorks (eCW)**, **NextGen**, **Kareo / Tebra**, **AdvancedMD**, **Office Ally**, and **MDLand**.
- **📩 Automated Lead Capture & Email Dispatch**: Integrated consultation form powered by **Resend** to automatically route practice inquiries directly to business email.
- **💬 Instant Multi-Channel Connect**: One-click WhatsApp direct chat link, phone dialing, and LinkedIn connection channels.
- **📱 Fully Responsive Clinical UI**: Mobile-first design built with Tailwind CSS, clinical color system, and modern typography.
- **⚡ SEO & Performance Optimized**: Server-rendered pages (SSR/SSG) built on the Next.js App Router for optimal search engine ranking.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, ES Modules)
- **Frontend**: [React](https://react.dev/), Modern JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Email Delivery**: [Resend](https://resend.com/) API
- **Deployment**: [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) (Continuous Deployment with Custom Domain & SSL)
